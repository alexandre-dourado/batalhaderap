# Prompts de Design para IA (Google Stitch / Midjourney / DALL-E)

Copie os prompts em inglês abaixo e cole na sua ferramenta de geração de imagens. Eles foram otimizados com os melhores termos para gerar telas de alta qualidade (UI/UX) com o tema Brutalista/Hip-Hop.

---

## 1. Tela de Batalha (Battle Live / Arena)
**Uso:** A tela principal onde os MCs se enfrentam, com o timer no meio e os cards de cada lado.

> **Prompt:**
> A highly detailed UI/UX design for a mobile and desktop web application called 'Batalha de Rap' (Rap Battle Tournament). The visual style is aggressive, urban, grunge, and brutalist. Dark theme, pitch black background (#090909) with high contrast neon accents like Acid Yellow, Cyberpunk Red, and Electric Blue. The typography is bold, massive, sans-serif, and condensed (like Impact or Oswald). 
> 
> Layout structure: A 1v1 battle arena. On the left side, MC A's profile card featuring a gritty hip-hop portrait avatar surrounded by a spray-painted circular frame and a glowing street-art crown. On the right side, MC B's profile card with a similar layout. In the exact center of the screen, a huge digital timer (0:45) painted like a street stencil over a torn caution tape background, and below it, a glowing vinyl record button for selecting beats. The bottom area features a control action bar with raw marker scribbles.
>
> Highly detailed, dribbble style, Behance winner, 8k resolution, flat UI elements mixed with realistic grunge textures, dripping paint, torn paper edges, spray paint fx, neon lighting.

## 2. Tela Inicial (Home)
**Uso:** A tela de boas-vindas do app, onde tem o logotipo gigante e o botão de criar torneio.

> **Prompt:**
> A highly detailed UI/UX design for the landing page of a web app called 'Batalha de Rap'. Aggressive urban brutalist style. Dark theme, pitch black background with neon green and acid yellow accents. In the center, a massive, stylized graffiti logo saying "BATALHA RAP". Below the logo, a huge call-to-action button that looks like a piece of torn cardboard with spray paint that says "NOVO TORNEIO". The background has subtle animated textures of concrete and dripping spray paint. Dribbble style UI, high contrast, clean but grungy, 8k, modern web design layout.

---

## 3. Tela de Setup (Inscrição de MCs)
**Uso:** A tela onde você cola a lista de MCs e eles aparecem em formato de grid (vários quadradinhos).

> **Prompt:**
> A highly detailed UI/UX design for a tournament setup screen. Brutalist dark UI style. The screen displays a grid of 16 participant cards. Each card contains a small hip-hop style character avatar and a name in bold condensed typography. Each card has a thick border in a unique neon color (cyan, magenta, yellow, lime). At the top, a raw text input field that looks like a street poster. The background is dark asphalt texture. Dribbble style, UI/UX, user interface, web app layout, 8k, brutalism, hip-hop aesthetic.

---

## 4. Tela de Chaveamento (Bracket View)
**Uso:** A tela das chaves do torneio (Oitavas, Quartas, Semifinal e Final).

> **Prompt:**
> A highly detailed UI/UX design for a tournament bracket screen (esports style but hip-hop themed). Brutalist dark UI. The screen shows a tournament tree progressing from left to right. The connecting lines are thick neon spray paint strokes. Each match node is a dark rectangular card containing two participant names in bold condensed font, separated by a neon "VS". The background is pitch black with subtle grunge textures. The current active match is highlighted with a glowing acid yellow border. Dribbble style, Behance winner, clear hierarchy, 8k resolution, UI design.
